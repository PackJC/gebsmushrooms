Mycology

Sixty-three fungi.
Know which is which.

36 are good to eat. 27 are look-alikes that will make you sick, and 10 of those kill: one whole mushroom is enough. Each one cooks, dries, burns and rots with its own look.

Edible. Safe raw or cooked.

Cook first. Upsets the stomach raw. Boil only: the woody brackets, safe only boiled fresh into a tea; dried, they burn in the pot like any dried food.

Poisonous. Food poisoning; charcoal tablets help.

Deadly. Organ damage that kills, slowly: one whole mushroom is enough, cooked or not. Only chelation tablets save you.

For server owners

Set up in minutes.

Nothing replaces vanilla. The fungi join the vanilla events that scatter mushrooms on the forest floor, so you add only the species you want.

  1. 1

    Install the mod

    Subscribe on the Workshop, place @gebsmushrooms beside the server executable, copy its key into keys/, and load -mod=@gebsmushrooms on the server. Every player needs the mod too.

  2. 2

    Start the server once

    The mod writes gebsmushrooms-types.xml, gebsmushrooms-cfgeconomycore.xml and two events files, gebsmushrooms-events-children.xml (all 63 fungi) and gebsmushrooms-events-children-edible.xml (the 36 edible ones), into $profile:Gebs/mpmissions/, beside Gebsfish's. Each file is stamped with the mod's version and a revision, and rewritten only when it's out of date: after an update that changes it, or when you delete it. Your edits stay put until then, so keep a copy of them.

  3. 3

    Register the types

    Copy gebsmushrooms-types.xml (the fungi, and the field guide's loot spawn) into mpmissions/<your mission>/gebsmushrooms/ and paste this block inside <economycore> in cfgeconomycore.xml.

    <ce folder="gebsmushrooms">
    <file name="gebsmushrooms-types.xml" type="types" />
</ce>
  4. 4

    Let them grow

    Pick one events file and merge its <child> lines into the vanilla Trajectory events in db/events.xml; don't merge both. They're already sorted by the woods each fungus grows in.

    Every fungus you add takes a share of those events' spawns. gebsmushrooms-events-children.xml adds all 63: vanilla's own mushrooms get much rarer and 27 poisonous species join them. gebsmushrooms-events-children-edible.xml adds only the 36 edible ones, so vanilla's mushrooms stay commoner and nothing new is poisonous. The lines below are the full set, with the poisonous and deadly ones marked.

  5. 5

    Test it

    Stop the server before editing and keep backups. Validate the XML, restart, and read the RPT and script logs. Values that suit Chernarus may not suit Sakhal, Livonia or a custom map.

Help

Questions.

Which mushrooms are dangerous?

Most of the poisonous ones cause food poisoning: vomiting, eased by charcoal tablets. The deadly ones damage the organs: one whole mushroom is enough to kill you over the next couple of hours, and only chelation tablets save you. A bite or two only makes you ill. Cooking, drying and rot don't help, except that boiling a false morel leaves only food poisoning. Several edible species, such as morels, must be cooked first, and anything burnt upsets the stomach. The Mushrooms section above marks every one.

Where do they grow?

On the forest floor near players, through the vanilla Trajectory events your server adds them to: conifer woods, broadleaf woods, or meadows and rich soil, depending on the species. Each one's details say where to look.

Can I cook and keep them?

Yes. Every fungus has its own baked, boiled, dried, burnt and rotten look, like vanilla's mushrooms, and cooks on a fireplace the same way. Dry the edible ones to keep them longer, but boil the tea fungi (turkey tail, hemlock reishi and chaga) fresh: dried food burns in the pot. The edible ones also bait a rabbit snare.

Is there a field guide in game?

Yes: Geb's Mushroom Field Guide, a field guide to all 63 fungi, grouped by how safe they are to eat, printed on a folded map. Look for it on the shelves of hunting spots and villages.

Where did the animals and garden crops go?

Geb's Mushrooms used to be part of Geb's Flora & Fauna. It is now three mods, so servers can pick what they want: Geb's Animals has the wildlife, and Geb's Plants has the garden crops and wild ginseng. The classnames haven't changed. If your server used the old mod, load all three to keep everything players already have.

Does every player need the mod?

Yes. It supplies the models, textures and item names.

Can I put it in a server pack?

No. Use the Workshop item, and a Steam Collection for your server's required mods.
